# Data Flywheel Map

> Score each loop 1-5. Your weakest loop is where competitors attack first.
> The four loops below are the M2 starting point - adapt if your product has 2 or 6 loops instead of 4.

## Flywheel Loops

| Loop | What It Measures | Score 1 | Score 5 | Score |
|------|------------------|---------|---------|-------|
| **Correction** | Do users fix AI outputs? Is that signal captured and reused? | No capture | Automated retraining | 3/5 |
| **Preference** | Does the product learn individual / team preferences over time? | Stateless | Deep personalization | 1/5 |
| **Domain Context** | Does usage in one area improve quality in adjacent areas? | Siloed | Cross-domain transfer | 4/5 |
| **Network** | Does each new user / team make the product better for everyone? | Isolated | Strong network effects | 5/5 |

### Correction Loop - 3/5
**What you capture today:** HITL touchpoints would capture signal as accepted/not accepted/flag for review.
**How it compounds:** Overtime, HITL could evolve where signals show positive acceptance or system changes if frequently not accepted.

### Preference Loop - _1/5
**What you capture today:** Okta sign-in could offer some level of role-based personalization in the future. Nothing in current prototype.
**How it compounds:** Preferences bases on role could drive process optimization and opportunity assessment for new features.

### Domain Context Loop - 4/5
**What you capture today:** If used across multiple domains or centralized, the context loop would capture different signals related to workflow and data collected. For example, Finance AI tracking would need additional rigor versus lower risk use cases where data and decisions are lower risk.
**How it compounds:** Optional fields and workflows to capture critical data for high risk. Right now, I think primary exploration is on the happy path.

### Network Loop - 5/5
**What you capture today:** thumbs up/down could be integrated with feedback. Also, with protoype, more white-glove would be used to ensure all feedback captured.
**How it compounds:** Over time, network feedback collection patterns would inform ongoing feedback collection.

**Total Flywheel Score: 13/20**
**Weakest Loop:** Preference Loop
**Fix for weakest loop:** Considerations for personalization should be considered.
