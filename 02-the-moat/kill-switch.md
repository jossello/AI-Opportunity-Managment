# Kill Switch Audit

## Vendor Dependency Assessment

| Dimension       | Current State                                                                                               | Risk Level | 48-Hour Action                                                                                                 |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Provider**    | OpenAI models used for classification, responsibility design recommendations, and governance summaries      | Medium     | Route requests through an abstraction layer and switch to Anthropic, Gemini, or enterprise-hosted alternatives |
| **Abstraction** | Model calls routed through a single AI service layer rather than directly embedded in application workflows | Low        | Update provider configuration without changing user workflows                                                  |
| **Routing**     | Most opportunities can be handled by lower-cost models with escalation only for high-risk reviews           | Low        | Shift additional workloads to cheaper models and reserve frontier models for governance edge cases             |
| **Eval**        | Governance outcomes, approval decisions, and pilot results stored independently from the model provider     | Low        | Continue measuring recommendation quality regardless of model vendor                                           |

---

## Portability Score

**Ready**

The product's primary asset is governance data, decision history, approval patterns, pilot outcomes, and organizational memory—not the underlying model provider.

Model replacement requires configuration changes rather than product redesign.

---

## If OpenAI Doubles Pricing Tomorrow

### 48-Hour Response

**Day 1**

* Freeze frontier model usage for low-risk opportunities
* Increase routing to lower-cost models
* Review actual inference spend versus labor costs

**Day 2**

* Activate secondary provider through abstraction layer
* Benchmark governance recommendation quality
* Adjust routing thresholds

### Business Impact

Minimal.

AI inference represents a small percentage of total operating cost.

Human review and governance activities remain the dominant cost drivers.

Expected gross margin impact: less than 5%.

---

## If OpenAI Ships a Competing Product

### What They Can Replicate

* Opportunity intake
* Classification workflows
* Governance forms
* Recommendation generation
* Executive dashboards

### What They Cannot Easily Replicate

#### Organizational Memory

Historical governance decisions, approval conditions, pilot outcomes, and accountability structures accumulated over years.

#### Governance Intelligence

Patterns linking work types, risk levels, autonomy recommendations, and successful deployments.

#### Enterprise Context

Knowledge of company-specific policies, review processes, risk tolerance, organizational structures, and decision-making practices.

#### Workflow Integration

Embedded relationships between Product, Security, Legal, IT, and business stakeholders.

#### Portfolio History

The complete record of which opportunities succeeded, failed, stalled, required escalation, or created business value.

---

## Defensible Asset

The moat is not the model.

The moat is the accumulated governance knowledge generated by the organization.

Competitors can replicate recommendation engines.

They cannot easily replicate years of AI decision history, approval rationale, pilot outcomes, accountability models, and institutional learning captured within the platform.
