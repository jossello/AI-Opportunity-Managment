## Board Pitch

### Thesis (1 Sentence)

Build the system of record for enterprise AI governance so organizations can evaluate, govern, and scale AI opportunities faster, safer, and at lower cost.

---

### The Case

#### 1. Why Now

Organizations are experiencing explosive growth in AI experimentation, but governance processes remain fragmented across spreadsheets, meetings, email, and disconnected workflows.

Without a structured operating model, companies cannot effectively manage risk, prioritize investments, or scale successful AI initiatives.

The gap between AI adoption and AI governance is becoming a strategic bottleneck.

---

#### 2. What's Defensible

The moat is not the model.

The moat is the accumulated governance intelligence generated by the organization.

Over time the platform captures:

* Governance decisions
* Approval conditions
* Risk assessments
* Responsibility models
* Pilot outcomes
* Accountability structures

This creates institutional memory competitors cannot easily replicate.

---

#### 3. The Economics

Current State:

* $406 governance cost per opportunity
* 30-day approval cycle
* Limited portfolio visibility

Future State:

* $126 governance cost per opportunity
* 7-day approval cycle
* Complete portfolio visibility

Expected Impact:

* 69% reduction in governance costs
* 4x increase in opportunity throughput
* Faster deployment of high-value AI initiatives

---

## The Risks

### 1. Trust / Failure Modes

Risk:

Incorrect classifications or governance recommendations.

Mitigation:

Human review remains mandatory for all high-risk opportunities.

Success Metric:

> 95% escalation recall for high-risk use cases.

---

### 2. Scale / Governance

Risk:

Governance becomes a bottleneck as AI adoption grows.

Mitigation:

Reuse governance patterns, automate low-risk reviews, and standardize decision frameworks.

Success Metric:

Opportunities reviewed per governance team member.

---

### 3. Competitive

Risk:

Microsoft, Atlassian, ServiceNow, or governance startups embed similar capabilities.

Mitigation:

Build proprietary governance intelligence and organizational memory rather than workflow automation alone.

Success Metric:

Percentage of opportunities using reusable governance patterns.

---

## The Ask

Approve investment in an enterprise AI governance operating system to establish a scalable process for evaluating, governing, and deploying AI opportunities across the organization.

Initial success will be measured by:

* Governance cost per opportunity
* Approval cycle time
* Pilot success rate
* AI opportunity throughput

---

## M1 Baseline vs. Now

### M1 Baseline

Build an AI-powered workflow platform that helps organizations intake, review, and approve AI opportunities.

### Now

Build the operating system that enables organizations to evaluate, govern, and scale AI safely.

The software is valuable, but the long-term asset is organizational intelligence: the accumulated decisions, governance patterns, pilot outcomes, and institutional learning that allow enterprises to deploy AI with increasing confidence and effectiveness over time.
