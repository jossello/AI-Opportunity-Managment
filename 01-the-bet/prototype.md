# The Prototype Bet

## What I Built
<!-- One sentence: what does this prototype demonstrate? -->
Hypothesis: A structured AI Opportunity Record can progressively capture and enrich information from business stakeholders, Product, AI PM, and governance reviewers, resulting in governance-ready decision packages that improve the speed, consistency, and quality of AI investment decisions without creating excessive process overhead.
ai
## Prototype Success Criteria

### 1. Business Stakeholder Usability

**Validation Question:** Can business stakeholders describe AI opportunities without needing AI expertise?

**Success Signal:** Opportunity intake can be completed without facilitator assistance.

---

### 2. Work Classification Consistency

**Validation Question:** Can Product consistently classify the work being proposed?

**Success Signal:** Similar opportunities produce similar work classifications and archetypes.

---

### 3. Responsibility Design Feasibility

**Validation Question:** Can the AI PM define autonomy, accountability, and escalation requirements?

**Success Signal:** Responsibility Design can be completed with clear ownership and governance recommendations.

---

### 4. Governance Decision Readiness

**Validation Question:** Can the AI Tools Review Huddle make a decision from the information provided?

**Success Signal:** Huddle members can approve, revise, reject, or escalate opportunities without significant rework or additional discovery.

---

### 5. Process Efficiency

**Validation Question:** Is the process lightweight enough to encourage adoption?

**Success Signal:** The opportunity record can be completed within an acceptable time investment and does not create excessive process overhead.

---

### 6. Decision Quality

**Validation Question:** Does the opportunity record improve decision quality?

**Success Signal:** Decisions are more consistent, explainable, and traceable than ad hoc AI requests.

---

### 7. Portfolio Visibility

**Validation Question:** Can opportunities be compared across functions using a common framework?

**Success Signal:** Opportunities can be grouped and reviewed by work type, archetype, autonomy level, risk level, and recommended path.

---

### 8. Early Risk Identification

**Validation Question:** Does the process surface governance concerns early enough?

**Success Signal:** Security, legal, data, and accountability concerns are identified before pilot approval.

---

### 9. Portfolio Management Support

**Validation Question:** Can the system support portfolio management, not just intake?

**Success Signal:** Opportunity records remain useful after approval and can be tracked through pilot, adoption, and portfolio review stages.

---

### 10. Governance-Ready Outputs

**Validation Question:** Does the operating model create governance-ready decision packages?

**Success Signal:** The output provides sufficient context, classification, responsibility design, and recommendations to support meaningful AI Tools Review Huddle decisions.
